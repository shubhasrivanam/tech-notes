## What?
In simple terms, SSH (Secure Shell) is a cryptographic protocol that allows us to communicate securely between one computer and another computer or server.

But what exactly does “securely” mean here?

Computers communicate with one another over networks, and the data traveling across those networks can potentially be intercepted by someone in the middle. Without proper security, an attacker could potentially capture and inspect the information being transmitted.

This is where SSH comes in.

SSH encrypts the communication between two computers, making the data traveling between them unreadable to anyone who might intercept it. This allows us to securely connect to and communicate with another computer or server without exposing the information being exchanged.

In other words, SSH creates a secure, encrypted channel between two machines, protecting the communication from potential eavesdroppers.
